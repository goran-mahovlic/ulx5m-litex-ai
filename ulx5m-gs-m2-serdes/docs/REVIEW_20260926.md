# ULX5M-GS ↔ ULX5M-M2 SerDes — review with direct JTAG on both boards

**Date:** 2026-09-26 · **Task:** TASK-5052 · **Author:** Jelena (Engineer, REGOČ) · **Requested by:** Goran (Telegram 26.09. 11:57)
**Lab:** the lab Raspberry Pi, two DirtyJTAG probes — `gs` (E660583883501E2C, busdev 1:16) and `m2` (E6613008E33AA42D, busdev 1:17). The only link between the boards is the SerDes cable. Lab rules: `docs/FPGA_LAB_ARHITEKTURA.md`.

> **Summary.**
> 1. **The GS↔M2 8b10b link works in both directions, bit-exact.** With the same CFGRST bitstream on both boards (`serdes_dut_CFGRST.bit`, CologneChip `serdes_lb.v`, normal mode, K28.5 + D10.2), both receivers decode exactly what the other side sends. Result: **180/180 samples DATA_OK** over 3 fresh SRAM loads × 2 boards × 30 JTAG reads.
> 2. **The lane polarity is swapped (P/N) in both directions.** Without `RX_POLARITY=1` every receiver sees D21.5 (0xB5 = ~0x4A) instead of D10.2. K28.5 is symmetric under inversion, so byte alignment still locks. That is why alignment "worked" while data looked wrong or absent.
> 3. **The CDR and alignment flags are not link-health indicators.** `RX_CDR_LOCKED` and `RX_BYTE_IS_ALIGNED` stay at 1 while the far transmitter is in electrical idle (rx80 = 0xFF…FF). The verdict must come from the RX_DATA content.
> 4. The July blocker "8b10b unobservable" (STANJE S14–S16) is closed. Every board is now the near device on its own probe, and the tools pick the probe by `FPGA_JTAG_BUSDEV`.
> 5. The CC_SERDES PRBS checker read through the regfile is still unusable: `RX_PRBS_ERR_CNT` is saturated at 0x7FFF regardless of polarity or 8b10b. BER needs a fabric checker (plan §6).

### 3.3 July builds with CFGRST (`01-serdes-link-N123`: `m2_tx_por_CFGRST` → `gs_rx_rstfix_CFGRST`)
- Both have `SERDES_TESTMODE=0` (0x5C=0x0001). JTAG can read, but regfile writes are ignored, so polarity cannot be fixed without a rebuild.
- gs RX: PLL 1, CDR 5/5, RST_DONE 5/5, **rx80 non-zero and changing** (PRBS-31 from m2). The July symptom "CDR locked but RX_DATA = 0" did **not** repeat with CFGRST.
- `RX_PRBS_ERR_CNT = 32767` (saturated), with `RX_PRBS_LOCKED = 1`.

### 3.4 PRBS through regfile overrides on the DUT (TESTMODE=1)
- `TX_PRBS_SEL=4` (PRBS-31) on both boards, `RX_PRBS_SEL=4`, `RX_PRBS_CNT_RESET`, polarity 1 and 0, 8b10b on and off through the OVR bits, and gs `RX_PMA_LOOPBACK`: **always `ERR_CNT = 0x7FFF`**.
- With 8b10b switched off by override, rx80 froze at `0x230000004FFFFFFF71C7` on both boards.
- Conclusion: STANJE S12 still holds. The regfile PRBS counter is not a BER instrument. The boards were returned to the proven DUT state (both PASS, 14:15).

## 7. Uncertainties (what was not proven)

- **BER is not measured.** 180/180 are JTAG snapshots of a static repeating pattern, not a count of errors on the line (plan P2–P3).
- The encoding of the `TX_DATA_OVR` override (`wr_regfile_tx_data`) is not decoded: the received word changes deterministically but is not equal to the one written. It does not affect the conclusions (the idle test and the fabric pattern prove direction and content).
- Where the inversion comes from (TX side, RX side, cable/M.2 adapter) is not determined. The effect is the same in both directions. The KiCad netlist of the chain (GS → CM4 IO → PCIe → M.2) could confirm it.
- The inherited m2 design at 13:59 (0x5C=0x0001, CDR 20/20) is unknown. It is not needed for the conclusions.
- The PRBS behaviour through overrides (rx80 freezes with 8b10b OFF) is not explained.
